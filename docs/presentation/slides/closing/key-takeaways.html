Key Takeaways


1. Personas bring engineering rigor to AI instructions.
Not prompts — operating manuals. Versioned, templated, validated.
2. Agent personas are useful for everyone.
Engineer your own personas - for both business and private use.
3. A shared ledger turns isolated sessions into a coordinated team.
Persistent memory + automatic handoffs + collective intelligence.
4. This approach works today, with tools you already have.
VS Code, Claude Code, Gemini, ChatGPT — personas work across all of them.
5. Structure closes the context gap. You still close the judgement gap.
A capable colleague who was never in the room — and never says they're unsure.
Five things to remember. Personas are engineering artifacts. They're for everyone, not just developers. The ledger creates a team. You can start today with what you already have. And the fifth one is the one I'd most want to survive the walk back to the desk: all of this reduces what the model doesn't know, and none of it replaces your judgement about whether the work is the right work.
